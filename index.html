FAQ

How fast is local delivery?

Most demo orders are shown as arriving within the selected delivery window. Actual shipping is simulated for this frontend demo.

When is shipping free?

Orders of $50 or more after discounts qualify for free shipping. Orders below that threshold pay $4.99 shipping.

Can I change my saved address?

Logged-in demo customers can edit saved addresses from My Account. Changes last only for the current browser session.

Are payments real?

No. Payment options are frontend-only demo choices and no external payment service is contacted.
